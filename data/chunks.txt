(15th July, 2024) 
 
The 43 rd Rice Scientists Meet was held on 15 th July, 2024, at Tamil Nadu Rice 
Research Institute, Aduthurai. Pre-review of individuals research projects, action 
taken on OFT, action plan projects were made by concerned Technical Directors in 
the concurrent session at TNAU, Coimbatore. Dr. V. Geethalakshmi,  Vice-
Chancellor chaired the meeting and emphasized on the popularization of newly 
released TNAU rice varieties for more horizontal spread. Madam insisted mainly on 
widening the genetic base, development of improved landraces, DSR management 
practices, sustainable soil fertility enhancem

It was quite popular in Himalayan Tarai of eastern Uttar Pradesh, India.
Tall stature causing lodging, long duration, and low yield and grain quality.
